=== SECOND SKIN: ENDINGS ===

Main ending families:

1. TRUE ENDING
   - One of 3 main endings.
   - Can be obtained through the main plot.
   - Main goal involves reaching $10M before a certain period, followed by a major post-$10M event and further choices.
   - True Ending is associated with the Player living under their own identity/name.
   - In the True Ending path, romantic attachment is not genuine love; it is more associated with lust, status, fame, family background, benefits, etc.
   - Love Merge is the only ending where the Player genuinely falls in love.

2. MIXED NEUTRAL ENDINGS
   - One main ending family.
   - Has variations caused by major choices during the game.
   - The core ending is similar, but the "flavor" changes based on choices. Think same dish with different flavor rather than completely different dishes.
   - Default/neutral route: if there is no plot twist and neutral choices are made during major choice moments, this leads toward the default path, currently described as ~90% at boards + the dream collage.

3. LOVE MERGE ENDING
   - One of the 3 main endings.
   - Requires a very specific Love DLC route.
   - Love DLC normally has little effect on the main plot.
   - Under the special route, the endgame provides Choice A/B:
       A = Love Merge ending.
       B = True Ending.
   - A merges the Love DLC timeline/story with the main plot.
   - The outcome is radically different from the True Ending, described as an "ocean vs sky" difference rather than right vs wrong.
   - This is the only ending where the Player genuinely falls in love.

Non-main / secret endings:

4. JAD'S SKIN
   - Secret/non-main ending.
   - Not labeled officially as a bad ending, but it functions as a bad outcome.
   - The Player never fully sheds Jad's identity.
   - The Player mixes their own personality into Jad over time while continuing to be perceived as Jad.
   - A major later event normally allows the Player to shed Jad's identity and leave with a new passport/name chosen by the Player. Jad's Skin prevents this trajectory.
   - The Player becomes increasingly trapped in Jad's identity and develops a major identity crisis, losing grip on who they are while trying to fit into the identity they inherited.
   - This blocks the True Ending even if the $10M goal is reached.

5. HUMAN HEART
   - Secret/non-main ending.
   - Exactly one ending, with no variants.
   - Requires a highly conditional chain of morally motivated choices.
   - During the main plot, some major events can be prevented by the Player.
   - The Player does NOT need to stop every event, only the events that morally should not happen.
   - These interventions are difficult and can be disadvantageous to the story.
   - They can alter the timeline even when doing so gives the Player no gameplay/story benefit.
   - Example concept: discovering a hidden report that a parent is critically ill and choosing to provide/save them despite the intended story trajectory.
   - Reaching $10M does not substitute for these moral choices and does not automatically produce the True Ending.
   - Core theme: choosing to prevent morally wrong outcomes even when doing so costs the Player and is not rewarded by the main plot.

=== SECOND SKIN: DAILY GAMEPLAY LOOP ===

Weekdays / class period:
- At the start of each weekday, except Friday and the weekend, the Player enters a class period.
- The class period is important for Love DLC progress and other social/opportunity progress.
- The main plot generally cannot progress during the class period.
- The Player can choose to skip the class period early in the game to access main-plot progression sooner.
- Skipping class grants +1 action.
- Skipping class can cause the Player to miss Love DLC progress, hidden details, opportunities, social development, or story-related information/triggers.
- Skipping class is a strategic tradeoff, not inherently a bad choice.
- As of Sept 1, attending class is currently favored when it provides Love DLC progression.

Daily actions:
- Each day has a limited action budget.
- Default action budget is 4 actions per day.
- After the available actions are used, the day ends.
- The Player can increase the number of available actions by working on Willpower through difficult or discipline-heavy activities such as gym or other hard tasks.
- Main plot events can occur during ordinary actions rather than only through explicitly labeled main-plot actions.
- Example: while doing diet groceries and searching for a card, the Player may discover special documents they were not supposed to see.
- Some plot changes and opportunities are sudden; others may be discoverable.
- Ordinary actions can therefore unexpectedly trigger or alter story events.

Time skip:
- Doomscrolling and watching movies/series are 0-action time-skip options.
- They advance the in-game day without consuming an action.
- They do not directly reduce Daily Rank.
- Their purpose is to let the Player fast-forward through time when desired.
- They are not intended to be hidden productivity penalties.

End of day:
- The final hour before sleep takes place on the rooftop.
- The Player reviews how the day went.
- The Player can see how White played the same day.
- Individual daily results matter because they feed the weekly report and progress graph.
- The primary official progress report is weekly rather than a day-number report.


=== SECOND SKIN: RANK SYSTEM ===

Daily Rank:
- Rank measures how well the available day was used, not simply how many actions were consumed.
- 4/4 actions does NOT automatically produce A or S.
- Rank considers factors such as:
   - Productivity and meaningful progress
   - Balance between areas of life
   - Self-discipline / doing difficult things
   - Important opportunities and story/relationship progress when reasonably available
   - Overall self-care and use of circumstances
- Rank should evaluate whether the Player made good use of the situation they were given rather than demanding maximum activity regardless of circumstances.
- Rest or low-action days can still rank well when appropriate to the circumstances.
- Rank and actual stat/progression changes are separate systems.

Ranks:
- S = exceptional day; excellent performance and clear competitive edge over the other run.
- A = very good day.
- B = solid/normal day.
- C = poor day.
- F = very poor / severely failed day.

S-rank competition:
- Only ONE of Ryu or White can receive S Rank on a given day.
- S Rank can only be awarded if BOTH Ryu and White independently qualify for A Rank.
- If one gets A and the other gets B or lower, the A player remains A; no S is awarded.
- If both get A, their meaningful overall progress is compared.
- The one who slightly outperforms the other receives S; the other remains A.
- A minimum meaningful difference should be used so microscopic numerical differences do not decide S.
- White getting B or lower is rare because White generally plays a near-best run.
- Therefore, if Ryu gets A while White gets B or lower, Ryu's performance is exceptionally strong relative to the benchmark even though the formal rank remains A.

- Weekly reports contain the overall stats/progress information rather than displaying a full day-by-day rank history.
- Weekly report can include overall rank average, graph movement, stats changes, money, Love DLC progress, story developments, Willpower, streak status, and other relevant weekly information.
- White can be compared against Ryu through the same weekly progress framework.
- The graph is a cumulative measure of performance quality, not a direct measurement of money, weight, XP, or another single stat.


=== SECOND SKIN: STREAK WILLPOWER ===

- Consistency across a week can create a Willpower streak bonus.
- If the Player maintains a strong week and finishes with an A or S weekly result, the next week starts with a permanent extra Willpower bonus.
- This bonus does not require separate Willpower actions to maintain during that next week.
- The streak bonus ends if the following week does not achieve the required A/S result.
- The purpose is to reward consistency and create a snowball effect for sustained good performance.

=== SECOND SKIN: WISDOM IMPLANT ===

Wisdom Implant:
- A special cheat that temporarily implants the consultant directly into the Second Brain's database.
- While active, the Player can ask an effectively unlimited number of questions and has unlimited consultation time.
- The consultation can provide expert/reference advice using supplied source material.
- The consultant's temporary knowledge is connected to the active Wisdom Implant session.
- When the Wisdom Implant tab/session is closed, memories related to the consultation disappear.
- This includes:
    • questions asked
    • answers/discussion
    • reasoning and consultation context
    • other memories created specifically through the temporary consultation.
- Neither the Player nor the Second Brain can recall the lost consultation after closure.
- The only surviving information is whatever was manually recorded before closing the session.
- Therefore, taking permanent notes before closing the implant is mandatory when useful information is discovered.
- Expert Advice is a related feature that provides expert consultation for limited time/questions but allows the Player to retain the wisdom afterward.
- Wisdom Implant's advantage is effectively unlimited consultation while active; its disadvantage is total memory loss after closure unless notes were preserved.

Completed Wisdom Implant session:
- Source supplied by consultant: Robert Greene, The Art of Seduction.
- Purpose: analyze the current Love DLC easy route.
- The consultation has been closed.
- Permanent notes were successfully recovered and incorporated into this Second Brain.

Recovered consultation principles:
1. IDEAL LOVER PRINCIPLE
   - Observe the individual rather than applying a universal attraction formula.
   - Subtle behavior, tone, gestures, expressions, looks, and reactions can reveal what a person values or emotionally seeks.
   - Useful application: observe what this specific girl responds positively or negatively to.
   - Lip-reading is useful for gathering contextual information, but observations should still be interpreted cautiously.

2. NATURAL PRINCIPLE
   - The source associates the Natural with spontaneity, sincerity, playfulness, comfort, and unpretentious interaction.
   - Childhood familiarity and emotional ease from long-standing familiarity are relevant to this archetype.
   - Potentially relevant because the current Love interest is the Player's long-standing best friend.
   - IMPORTANT: "Natural" is an analytical fit, NOT a confirmed game classification of the girl.

3. ENTER THEIR SPIRIT
   - Understand another person's world, participate in things they enjoy, adapt to their emotional rhythm, and meet them where they are.
   - Useful non-manipulative interpretation:
       understand the person's interests, moods, values, and perspective before trying to influence them.
   - Shared experiences and emotional attunement may therefore be more valuable than generic attraction techniques.

4. PAY ATTENTION TO DETAIL
   - Notice specific preferences and details about another person.
   - Small, personally meaningful gestures can matter more than generic or expensive gestures because they demonstrate genuine attention.
   - Money/spending should not be confused with emotional attentiveness.

Toxic/manipulative principles identified:
- The source also contains techniques involving uncertainty, strategic withdrawal, mixed signals, jealousy/triangles, and other forms of psychological manipulation.
- These may theoretically increase attraction or pursuit, but they are not equivalent to genuine love.
- "Give Them Space to Fall" involves withdrawal causing a person who is pursuing heavily to invest more of their own energy and potentially pursue more strongly.
- The Player appears to have accidentally used a mild version of this already by withdrawing/reducing attention, after which the girl's signaling behavior appeared to intensify.
- This is an observed game situation, not proof that the technique caused her behavior.
- Deliberate hot/cold manipulation is NOT currently recommended because mutual attraction is already extremely strong.
- Artificial uncertainty could create unnecessary damage rather than solving an attraction deficit.
- Toxic techniques can be studied as reference material when evaluating future game choices, but must remain distinguished from healthy relationship-building and from confirmed Love Merge mechanics.
