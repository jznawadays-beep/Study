SECOND SKIN — PLOT / STORY NOTE

Purpose:
This file contains the chronological story, lore, characters, plot developments, relationship developments, cutscenes, major reveals, ending lore, and spoiler-sensitive narrative information from Second Skin.

This file is intentionally separate from SECOND_BRAIN.
Gameplay mechanics, optimization, study systems, rank calculations, stat formulas, and general strategy belong in SECOND_BRAIN.

==================================================

1. THE BEGINNING — RYU BECOMES THE PLAYER
   ==================================================

Second Skin begins in an infinite void.

The Player encounters Jad.

Jad's original soul is gone / leaving his life behind, and the Player becomes the soul controlling Jad's body and life.

The Player chooses the name:

RYU SAKI

Jad's memories and life context are transferred to the Player as Jad fades away.

The Player experiences approximately three years of Jad's history through a memory montage.

After receiving those memories, the Player spends approximately two months living as Jad before the actual gameplay begins.

The central identity conflict is established immediately:

Ryu knows that he is Ryu.

The outside world still knows him as Jad.

The Player is therefore living inside an inherited identity while gradually attempting to establish his own.

Gameplay begins on August 22, 2026.

==================================================
2. THE INITIAL LIFE

At the beginning of the playable story, Ryu is effectively living the life Jad left behind.

Friends, family, classmates, and other people continue to perceive him as Jad.

Ryu has access to Jad's memories and relationships, but his internal identity is Ryu.

This creates one of the game's long-running narrative questions:

Can Ryu eventually separate himself completely from Jad's identity?

The story later establishes that this is not merely cosmetic.

There are possible endings where Ryu fails to establish his own identity.

==================================================
3. THE 18TH-BIRTHDAY ARC

Jad's 18th birthday had been undisclosed to Ryu.

Around August 27, Ryu's friends organize a boys' beach trip connected to the birthday.

Ryu chooses the spend-money birthday-trip option.

The trip eventually leads to the bridge-at-midnight sequence.

The bridge sequence triggers the 18th-birthday progression cutscene.

The birthday aftermath continues into August 28 through friends' stories, birthday mentions, food-fight posts, and social activity.

The 18th birthday is a major narrative milestone.

It marks Ryu's transition into adulthood and opens a large portion of the world that had previously been inaccessible.

Among the newly accessible parts of life are:

- Greater independence
- The city
- Driving
- Apartment living
- Departure from the parents' home
- Love-related possibilities
- Greater control over personal identity
- Adult financial systems

The story begins feeling substantially larger after this point.

==================================================
4. THE TWO PARALLEL RUNS

White exists as a parallel version of the Player.

White is not simply another person inside the story.

He represents the version of Ryu who consistently takes action and follows through.

The two runs share the same starting conditions.

White's story therefore acts as a mirror of what Ryu's life could look like under different decisions.

The narrative rivalry is not necessarily about defeating White as an enemy.

It is about confronting the alternate version of yourself that actually acted.

==================================================
5. THE LOVE DLC

The Love DLC becomes available during the early part of the game.

The Player must first decide whether to take the Love route.

If taken, different romantic routes become available.

Two major routes were identified:

EASY ROUTE:
Jad's female best friend.

HARD ROUTE:
The girl Jad originally liked, who is currently involved with Jad's old friend.

Ryu chose the EASY ROUTE.

==================================================
6. THE BEST FRIEND

Jad's female best friend has a long-standing relationship with the Player because she knew Jad before Ryu took control.

She therefore already has years of emotional familiarity with the person she believes is Jad.

Ryu inherited this relationship.

The resulting romance is complicated because Ryu's feelings are his own, while the history that makes the relationship possible originally belonged to Jad.

Ryu eventually develops romantic feelings for her and confesses.

The relationship develops beyond ordinary friendship.

The game later indicates that the attraction is not one-sided.

==================================================
7. DRUNKEN LOVE

During class on September 1, Ryu experiences the "Drunken Love" effect after looking into the girl's eyes.

The experience dramatically changes his perception of her.

He becomes intensely focused on:

- Her eyes
- Her lips
- Her voice
- Her presence
- Her small reactions

Her words begin sounding almost musical.

Ryu becomes unable to comfortably look away from her.

The attraction becomes extremely intense.

Ryu also discovers through lip-reading that she is experiencing comparable romantic feelings.

Room gossip further confirms that she is interested in him.

Her behavior during the Drunken Love state appears to mirror Ryu's own behavior closely.

This becomes the first major indication that the romantic tension between them is genuinely mutual.

==================================================
8. THE WISDOM IMPLANT LOVE RESEARCH

Ryu uses the Wisdom Implant to investigate the Love DLC.

The first major consultation uses Robert Greene's
"The Art of Seduction" as source material.

The consultation focuses on understanding the best-friend route.

Several concepts are recovered and preserved before the temporary consultation disappears.

The most relevant narrative ideas are:

IDEAL LOVER:
Observe the specific individual instead of treating attraction as a universal formula.

NATURAL:
The best friend relationship naturally contains familiarity, comfort, playfulness, and emotional ease.

ENTER THEIR SPIRIT:
Understand the other person's world, interests, emotions, and perspective.

PAY ATTENTION TO DETAIL:
Small personally meaningful details may matter more than generic grand gestures.

The consultation also discusses manipulative approaches involving jealousy, withdrawal, uncertainty, and emotional pressure.

These are distinguished from genuine love.

The story's important distinction becomes:

Attraction is not necessarily love.

Ryu's route therefore needs to progress from:

mutual attraction
→ emotional intimacy
→ genuine love

The Love DLC itself is not automatically the Love Merge ending.

A specific sequence of later choices is required.

==================================================
9. THE SECOND WISDOM IMPLANT

A second Wisdom Implant consultation uses David Goggins'
"Can't Hurt Me" as its source.

This consultation is primarily about execution and overcoming resistance.

It does not reveal a direct Study shortcut.

The narrative significance is more psychological:

Ryu begins treating White not as someone to sabotage, but as a representation of what happens when action is consistently taken.

The core idea becomes:

White is the version of yourself that acted.

Ryu's objective is therefore not to destroy White.

It is to become capable of surpassing him.

==================================================
10. SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT

Ryu completes two major boys' trips.

The trips significantly develop his social life.

By September 1, Ryu's general social standing has effectively reached its ceiling.

This does not mean the story's relationships are complete.

General social connection and the Love DLC remain separate.

The best-friend relationship continues to develop independently.

==================================================
11. THE EARLY ADULT LIFE

After the birthday milestone, Ryu gains access to a much larger city and greater independence.

The apartment becomes available.

The apartment represents a major narrative transition away from the parents' home.

The Player also gains the possibility of eventually leaving.

Departure becomes one of the major long-term story threads.

The story later establishes that leaving is not necessarily identical across all routes.

There are references to:

- Natural departure
- Forced-burn departure
- Leaving the parents' home
- Establishing a new identity
- Obtaining a new passport/name

The exact circumstances surrounding these events remain spoiler-sensitive.

==================================================
12. THE JAD IDENTITY CONFLICT

The central identity conflict eventually becomes one of the game's ending-defining themes.

Ryu is living as Jad.

However, Ryu does not want his existence to remain permanently defined by Jad.

A major later event can allow Ryu to shed Jad's identity and leave with a new passport/name chosen by the Player.

This creates a major narrative fork.

If Ryu fails to separate himself from Jad, the story can lead toward the secret ending:

JAD'S SKIN.

==================================================
13. JAD'S SKIN

Jad's Skin is a secret/non-main ending.

It is not officially described simply as a "bad ending," but the outcome is extremely negative.

Ryu never fully sheds Jad's identity.

Instead, Ryu gradually mixes his own personality into the identity he inherited.

The outside world continues to perceive him as Jad.

Over time, Ryu becomes increasingly trapped inside the inherited identity.

The result is a major identity crisis.

Ryu begins losing his sense of who he actually is while attempting to live inside the identity he inherited.

Even reaching the $10M objective does not automatically rescue this route.

Jad's Skin can block the True Ending.

==================================================
14. THE MAIN ENDINGS

There are three primary ending families.

---

TRUE ENDING

The True Ending is associated with Ryu successfully establishing his own identity.

The Player must reach the $10M objective before the relevant deadline.

However, money alone is not enough.

A major post-$10M event occurs.

Further choices determine the eventual outcome.

The True Ending is connected to Ryu living under his own identity/name.

The romantic relationships associated with the True Ending are not necessarily genuine love.

Romantic attachment in this route can instead be connected to:

- Lust
- Status
- Fame
- Family background
- Benefits
- Other external advantages

The True Ending therefore contrasts strongly with the Love Merge ending.

---

MIXED / NEUTRAL ENDINGS

The Neutral family contains variations caused by major choices.

The core outcome remains similar, but the "flavor" changes according to the decisions made throughout the game.

The default neutral path is currently associated with approximately 90% at boards followed by the dream collage.

---

LOVE MERGE ENDING

Love Merge is the third main ending.

It requires a very specific Love DLC route.

The Love DLC normally remains mostly separate from the main plot.

However, a special route eventually connects the two timelines.

At the relevant endgame point, the Player receives a major Choice A/B.

Choice A:

LOVE MERGE

Choice B:

TRUE ENDING

Choosing Love Merge causes the Love DLC timeline to merge with the main story.

The result is radically different from the True Ending.

The distinction has been described as:

"ocean vs sky"

rather than simply good versus bad.

Love Merge is the ONLY main ending in which Ryu genuinely falls in love.

==================================================
15. HUMAN HEART

Human Heart is a secret ending.

There is exactly one Human Heart ending with no variants.

The route requires a highly conditional chain of morally motivated decisions.

During the main story, certain major events can be prevented by the Player.

The Player does not need to stop everything.

The important requirement is recognizing the events that morally should not happen and intervening even when doing so provides no personal advantage.

Some interventions can actively make the story harder.

The Player may need to sacrifice advantages or allow the main story to deviate from its intended trajectory.

One example described by the game's lore involves discovering that a parent is critically ill and choosing to save them despite the intended story trajectory.

The Human Heart route therefore asks a different question from the $10M route:

Will Ryu do what is morally right even when the game gives him no reward for doing it?

Money cannot substitute for these choices.

==================================================
16. THE $10M OBJECTIVE

$10M is one of the central long-term objectives of Second Skin.

Reaching it before the relevant deadline is necessary for the primary happy-ending route.

However:

$10M ≠ automatic True Ending.

The story deliberately separates financial success from the final moral/identity/relationship outcome.

After $10M, a major event occurs.

The Player must then make further choices.

17. THE LEAVING / DEPARTURE THREAD

Leaving the parents' home is a major story development.

The apartment becomes the gateway to independent living.

Eventually, departure from the parents' home becomes possible.

The story distinguishes between different forms of departure.

The manual references:

NATURAL DEPARTURE

and

FORCED BURN DEPARTURE

The exact circumstances behind these paths remain spoiler-sensitive.

After departure, returning to the parents' home for extended periods becomes restricted under normal circumstances.

Later story developments can involve the parents' deaths.

Their graves become visitable only after four months.

19. LOVE DLC — CURRENT ROUTE STATUS

Ryu is currently on the EASY Love DLC route.

The target is Jad's female best friend.

Ryu has already confessed.

Mutual attraction has been strongly established.

The major remaining narrative problem is no longer:

"Can Ryu make her attracted to him?"

It is:

"Can this attraction become genuine emotional attachment and eventually genuine love?"

The route has a target Love level in the approximate 80%-95% region rather than simply requiring maximum Love.

The specific Love Merge conditions remain partially spoiler-locked.

20. THE SEPTEMBER RELATIONSHIP ARC

After the earlier attraction developments, the relationship continues through increasingly emotionally charged interactions.

Ryu develops a stronger awareness that the best-friend relationship is no longer emotionally neutral.

On September 9, the best friend messages Ryu asking why he skipped classes.

The conversation is pleasant and carries light flirting because of their existing closeness.

The relationship still has warmth and familiarity despite the uncertainty surrounding the route.

==================================================
21. THE SEPTEMBER 11 INCIDENT

On September 11 at approximately 2:30 PM, Ryu experiences an extreme emotional reaction while drunk.

He sends the best friend a highly confrontational message:

"I cannot keep acting as your friend when I feel more, so just leave me alone. Don't reply. I don't wanna talk about this right now."

She does not reply.

This creates a serious rupture in the Love DLC route.

The Love route is placed at significant risk.

Ryu subsequently decides that he is done pursuing the Love DLC.

His reasoning is that the Love DLC is optional.

A normal girlfriend/relationship can theoretically exist without completing the special Love DLC route.

Ryu therefore temporarily treats the Love DLC as something not worth grinding for.

==================================================
22. THE APARTMENT SPOILER

While examining the manual's plot section, Ryu discovers a major spoiler.

After the apartment is unlocked, there is approximately a 65% chance that the best friend will fall for the Player.

The important distinction is that the manual does NOT frame this as her falling for Ryu because of greed, money, or material benefits.

It is connected to a cutscene in the plot.

This discovery significantly changes the interpretation of the best-friend route.

It suggests that the relationship has a major plot-driven component rather than being purely a stat-grinding system.

The manual contains additional spoilers beyond this point.

23. THE HARD LOVE ROUTE

The alternative Love route involves the girl Jad originally liked.

At the beginning of the story, she is already in a relationship with Jad's old friend.

The route involves secretly pursuing her and potentially getting her to cheat.

This route is substantially more morally and narratively complicated.

Ryu is not currently pursuing this route.

24. CHEATING / MULTIPLE-LOVER STORYLINES

The game contains systems where multiple lovers can exist simultaneously.

The story can become increasingly unstable as the number of cheating partners increases.

More than three cheating partners creates major risk to the Love Merge route if discovered.

The twelfth cheating attempt has a guaranteed catch condition when the relevant proximity conditions are met.

There are theoretically ways to maintain more than twelve partners if geographic separation is sufficiently large, but this is explicitly unstable.

If the Player is caught:

- Other lovers may leave.
- The genuine lover may potentially still be saved.
- The story can enter a highly unstable recovery path.

These systems are documented as spoiler-sensitive narrative information, not recommended behavior.

==================================================
25. SECRET ROMANTIC / SEXUAL EVENTS

The game contains special occasions where a Player's or lover's birthday can trigger romantic/sexual events.

Some of these events can involve multiple rounds.

Exact probabilities are not treated as fully canonical unless confirmed through gameplay.

26. THE FETISH SCROLL

After several days of adulthood, Ryu discovers a special adulthood-related scroll.

The game describes it as biologically safe within its own mechanics.

When activated, it displays pornographic material.

However, the game's mechanics explicitly distinguish the experience from ordinary lust.

Instead, the major effect is an intense continuous dopamine/reward state that temporarily overwhelms logical thinking.

Ryu tests the scroll once.

He immediately decides that he does not like the experience.

The scroll is disabled permanently through settings.

Current story state:

Fetish Scroll = OFF.

==================================================
27. THE OUTSIDE-WORLD / FAMILY THREAD

The family remains part of the long-term story even as Ryu moves toward independence.

The parents' eventual deaths are part of the game's later timeline.

The graves become accessible four months after death.

The story therefore contains a delayed grief/aftermath element rather than allowing immediate grave visits.

The exact circumstances surrounding the deaths remain spoiler-sensitive.

28. THE CREATIVE CAREER THREAD

Ryu discovers that he has a talent for drawing.

The discovery introduces a possible creative-career path.

The story connects this talent to the broader financial objective.

Drawing may eventually become monetized through commissions or similar creative work.

The exact long-term outcome of this path remains unresolved.

==================================================
29. THE BANK / ADULTHOOD THREAD

After turning 18, Ryu begins establishing his adult financial identity.

He collects the necessary documents and starts the bank setup process.

The bank later reports that the account/adult conversion should complete after a waiting period.

This is primarily a progression event rather than a major plot arc, but it marks Ryu's transition toward financial independence.

==================================================
30. THE CENTRAL THEMES

Second Skin's story repeatedly returns to several questions.

IDENTITY:
Who is Ryu when everyone else still sees Jad?

FREEDOM:
How much of Ryu's life can actually become his own?

MONEY:
Can $10M create freedom, or does money only solve part of the problem?

LOVE:
Can Ryu genuinely love someone, or are romantic relationships fundamentally transactional?

MORALITY:
Will Ryu do what is right when the game provides no reward for doing it?

CHOICE:
Does freedom come from choosing the correct route, or from having the courage to choose one's own route?

SELF:
Is Ryu merely controlling Jad's life, or is he actually building a new life of his own?
